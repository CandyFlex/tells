# About Ridgeline Bike Works

## Our Story

Ridgeline Bike Works started in 2009 in a converted garage on Alder Street, when Tom and Maria Castellano decided that Pine Hollow deserved a bike shop that treated every rider like a neighbor. What began as a weekend repair stand has grown into a full-service shop — but the heart of it hasn't changed. We're still family-owned, still fixing flats while you wait, and still happy to talk trails for as long as you'd like.

Today, the shop is run by the second generation of Castellanos alongside a small team of mechanics who ride the same roads and trails you do. Nestled at the foot of the Ridgeline trail system, we've had a front-row seat as Pine Hollow has grown into a vibrant cycling destination, and we're proud to have played a part in that story.

## More Than a Bike Shop

Ridgeline isn't just a place to buy a bike — it's a gathering place for the local riding community. On any given Saturday morning, you'll find commuters, weekend warriors, and first-time riders swapping stories over coffee before the group ride rolls out. We believe that cycling is for everyone, and we've built our shop around that idea.

Whether you're looking for your first mountain bike, tuning up a road bike that's been in the family for decades, or just need a tube and some friendly advice, you'll get the same honest, no-pressure service every time.

## What We Offer

- **Expert Repairs:** From quick adjustments to full overhauls, our certified mechanics handle every job with meticulous attention to detail. Most repairs are finished within 48 hours.
- **Quality Bikes:** We carry a carefully curated selection of mountain, road, gravel, and kids' bikes from brands we trust and ride ourselves.
- **Professional Fitting:** A properly fitted bike makes all the difference. Our fitting service helps you ride longer, faster, and more comfortably.
- **Rentals and Demos:** Visiting Pine Hollow? Our rental fleet lets you experience the trails on a high-quality, well-maintained bike.
- **Community Rides:** Free weekly group rides for all skill levels, because riding is better together.

## Our Values

Our commitment to the community goes beyond the shop floor. Every year, we partner with Pine Hollow Elementary to refurbish donated bikes for local kids, and we volunteer with the Ridgeline Trail Alliance to help build and maintain the trails that make this area so special. We see these efforts as a crucial part of who we are, not an extra.

We also believe in doing things the right way. That means honest recommendations, fair prices, and never selling you something you don't need. If a $15 part will solve your problem, that's what we'll suggest — even if there's a shinier option on the wall.

## Come Say Hello

At the end of the day, Ridgeline Bike Works is about people. It's about the kid who learns to ride without training wheels, the retiree discovering gravel riding, and the racer chasing a personal best. Whatever your goals are, we're here to help you reach them.

Stop by the shop at 214 Alder Street, give us a call, or join us for a Saturday ride. We can't wait to meet you — and your bike.
